A party of hiking enthusiasts sponsored by the M.I.T. Outing Club took an experimental hiking trip in the White Mountains last weekend. This group, led by Professor Dean Peabody, of the department of Civil Engineering, and Albert A. Sargent, '39, scaled the headwall of Huntington Ravine on Mt. Washington.

The men were equipped with crompons, ice axes, and skiis. They stayed in the Rocky Gulch hut of the Appalachian Mountain Club.

**Business Meeting**

A general business meeting of the Outing Club was held on Wednesday, March 29. Plans for the rest of the Spring and next year were discussed. Ski movies of Hannes Schneider were shown to members of the club.
