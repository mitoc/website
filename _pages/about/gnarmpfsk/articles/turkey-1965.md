The MIT Outing Club will host a slide presentation of mountaineering in the Cascades and Bugaboos, November 18, at 8 p.m. in the Bush Room (10-105). Admission is free.

On Nov. 25-27 MITOC will conduct its annual "Turkey-in-a-Tent." Winter-mountaineering, climbing, and Thanksgiving dinner on Mt. Katahdin, Baxter State Park, Maine, highlight the event. Information is posted on the Building 2 Bulletin Board or may be obtained from Shelby Hildebrand: 868-9052 or dl 0-552.
