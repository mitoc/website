The FinBoard also has the power to make loans and grants. In the minutes above the Tech Show loan is mentioned. Recently the Outing Club was granted $600 for the construction of a ski lodge. This falls under the FinBoard by-laws as grant "... to be a benefit to the undergraduate community."
