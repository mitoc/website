The MIT Outing Club is planning its first expedition of the year, a rock climbing expedition at the Quincy Quarries on Sunday, Sept. 23. Beginners are welcome as this is mainly an instruction trip. Bring sneakers and a lunch. Sign up at the Building 2 bulletin board. Meet at 9:00 a.m. on the front steps of Walker Memorial. Cost is 50 cents for transportation and 25 cents rope fee.
