Mountain climbers of the Outing Club will make their first post-war trip to Joe English Mountain next Sunday, October 27. This is a trip for experienced, but not necessarily expert, climbers.

The party will leave Walker Memorial at 8:00 A.M. and plans an all day outing. Members should bring lunches. Transportation will cost about $1.00 per man.
