Results of the Outing Club elections have just been announced. These men were elected at meeting held last Monday but were just made public. The officers for the coming year are: President, G. W. Elkins '25; Vice-President, H. N. Sachs '25; Secretary-Treasurer, G. C. Roberts '25; Club Council, E. E. Piepho '25, W. C. Ames G, and Edgar O'Neil '26.
