Camelot, MITOC's cabin near Rumney, NH, has been the club's home base for over 50 years. Camelot sees over 1000 person-nights each year and is where many members take their first MITOC trip. The cabin was built in 1973, largely by MIT undergrads, and won't stay safe to sleep in much longer.
